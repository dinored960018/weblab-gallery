                                    Loading rules

                                    1. Bone dry for bisque. Hold the piece to your cheek. Cool means damp.
                                    2. Glaze 6 mm clear of the foot. 12 mm for anything marked runs 4 mm or more, and a cookie underneath.
                                    3. Mark the base. Your initials and the glaze code in underglaze pencil.
                                    4. Shelf by 17:00. Late work waits for the next firing.
                                    5. Collect within 14 days. After that, pieces move to the back shelf.
                                    6. Studio closed for the holidays. First loads after the break: Monday 4 January.
