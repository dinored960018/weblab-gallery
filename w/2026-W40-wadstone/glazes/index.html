Reading a tile

Bare top
Unglazed clay. Shows the body colour after firing.
Top line
First dip, 3 seconds. The glaze thins at the line and breaks to its edge colour.
Ridges
Three pressed lines. Glaze pools in the groove and thins on the edge above it.
Lower half
Second dip. Twice the thickness, so the colour you get on the inside of a bowl.
Shine
Drawn as a window reflection. Glossy is sharp, satin is soft, matte has none.
