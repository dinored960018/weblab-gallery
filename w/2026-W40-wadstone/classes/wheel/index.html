Wheel throwing

Centre, open, pull and trim. Cylinders in week one, mugs with handles by week five.

Length
6 weeks
When
Tuesday or Thursday, 18:30–21:00
Price
£260 a seat
For
Beginners and people who threw years ago

Week by week

Included

  • 12.5 kg buff stoneware
  • All cone 6 glazes from the library
  • Bisque and glaze firings
  • Aprons and tools

  1. Wedging and centring

    Wedge 1 kg balls, centre on the wheel, open a flat floor.

  2. Cylinders

    Pull walls to 10 cm. Cut one in half to see wall thickness.

  3. Bowls

    Open wide, compress the floor, collar the rim.

  4. Trimming

    Trim feet on last week’s bowls at leather hard.

  5. Mugs and handles

    Pull handles, attach at leather hard.

  6. Glazing

    Dip in two library glazes. Pieces go into kiln A.
