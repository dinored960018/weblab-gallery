About this site

Form A0 · Notice

Demonstration site. The office, the records, the surnames and the prices are made up.

Notice

Bookings, slips, ordersNothing is sent or reserved. Bookings and slips stay in this browser tab and go when it is closed.
RecordsNo real register, minute book or person is described. Titles follow the pattern of county record office lists.
Contactenquiries@marrowby.example is a reserved example address.

Where the numbers come from

30-year closureThe usual range for records naming living people in English local archives. Not legal advice.
Shelf lengthOne standard archive box takes about 0.1 m of shelf.
PricesSet inside the range charged by county record offices in 2025–26.
Desks freeWorked out from the date, so the same date always gives the same number.
