In the reading room

What you can bring
ItemRule
PencilsYes. Pens of any kind stay in the lockers.
Laptop, tablet, phoneYes, on silent. Cameras with a permit only.
Notebooks and loose paperYes. Bags and coats go in the lockers.
Food and drinkNo. Water in the lobby.
Reader ticketBring photo ID and proof of address on your first visit. The ticket is free and lasts four years.

Getting here

BusRoutes 31 and 36 stop at Corn Exchange, 80 m from the door. Every 20 minutes Tuesday to Saturday.
TrainMarrowby station is 900 m away, about 12 minutes on foot along Kiln Street.
ParkingKiln Street car park, 140 spaces, pay and display, £1.20 an hour. Two blue badge bays at the door.
Step-freeRamp at the side door. The reading room and lockers are on the ground floor.
