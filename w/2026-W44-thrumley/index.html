    How a price is built

    1. 1

      Volume sets the van and crew. Up to 9 m³ a small van, up to 24 m³ medium, up to 38 m³ large. Over 38 m³ two vans.

    2. 2

      Hours. 0.16 h per m³ to load and unload with a crew of three, driving at 25 mph, rounded up to the half hour. Never below the van minimum.

    3. 3

      Extras. £1.60 a mile per van over 20 mi, £16 a floor without a lift, packing £14 per m³.

    4. 4

      Date. Saturday +15 %, Sunday +20 %, last two days of the month +10 %, on labour and stairs.
