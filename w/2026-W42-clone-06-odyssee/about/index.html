About

Sorrel & Vane was set up around a plain rule: treat only what the evidence supports. That means proven methods, a lasting look rather than trends, and a working relationship that continues for years. As your priorities change with age, we adjust the plan with you.

Your Sorrel & Vane clinical team

Dr Tomas Hallowell

Medical Director

MBBS, MSurg, Fellow in Cosmetic Medicine

Email

Dr Hallowell trained in emergency and trauma surgery before moving into facial medicine. He holds fellowships in cosmetic medicine and teaches injection safety to junior clinicians twice a year.

His clinical standard is simple: no treatment without a clear reason and a written plan. He reviews complex and corrective cases referred by other clinics and uses ultrasound guidance to map vessels before injecting.

He leads the laser and energy-device programme, building gradual, low-downtime courses for full-face renewal.

He consults in English and Spanish, and his focus is results that look natural at conversational distance.

RN Ines Varga

Cosmetic Nurse

Registered Nurse (RN)

Email

Ines has 14 years of nursing experience, most of it in intensive care, where she led a night team of twelve. That background shapes her focus on patient safety and careful technique.

She completed postgraduate training in cosmetic medicine and now works across dermal fillers, muscle relaxants, platelet-rich plasma and body contouring.

Ines plans each course with the patient at the table, explains every step before it happens, and keeps photo records at each review.

Clarity comes before treatment. Book a consultation to start your plan, built on evidence, precision and close follow-up.
