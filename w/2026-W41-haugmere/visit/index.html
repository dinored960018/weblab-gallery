Visit

East Shore Road, Haugmere, Haugby HB9 4HM. On the east shore, 14 m from the water.

Getting here

Bus
Route 41 from Haugby station, every 30 minutes until 22:10. Get off at East Shore, 200 m walk.
Bike
18 covered stands by the gate. The lakeside path is flat from town, 6 km.
Car
12 spaces, £2 for 3 hours. Full most weekend evenings; the overflow is the village hall, 600 m.
On foot
The east shore path from the boathouse, 25 minutes.
