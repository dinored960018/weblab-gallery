How it is worked out

A starting point, not an answer. Ride it, then go 5 psi either way.

  1. Load on each wheel. Rider plus bike, 60 % on the rear and 40 % on the front, in pounds.
  2. Pressure for 15 % tyre drop. Frank Berto’s measurements, fitted as psi = 153.6 × load ÷ width1.5785 − 7.17.
  3. Surface. Worn road × 0.93, gravel × 0.85, mixed off-road × 0.78.
  4. Tubeless. × 0.92, because there is no tube to pinch.
  5. Limits. Never above the maximum printed on the tyre, and never above 72 psi (5 bar) on a hookless rim.

Check the limits printed on your tyre and rim. The lower of the two wins.
