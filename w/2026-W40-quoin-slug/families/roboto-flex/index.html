Credits and source

  • Google commissioned Font Bureau to extend Roboto into a range of weights, grades, widths and optical sizes, with emphasis on large screens.
  • Thirteen axes in one file: three registered (weight, width, optical size), slant, grade and eight parametric axes that set stroke thickness, counter width and vertical proportions.
  • Font Bureau first showed parametric axes in Amstelvar Alpha (2017).
