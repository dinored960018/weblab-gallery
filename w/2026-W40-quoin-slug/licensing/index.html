Questions

What counts as a user?

One person who can open the font on their computer. Five designers sharing two machines count as five users.

Do I need a web license for a PDF?

No. A PDF, an image or a printed page is covered by the desktop license.

Can I mix license types?

Yes. Add desktop, web and app to the same family; each is priced on its own tier and added up.

What does the bundle include?

All six families on the same tiers, 40% below the sum of the six.

Why are these fonts free elsewhere?

They are. All six are published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and can be downloaded from Google Fonts at no cost. Quoin & Slug is a practice site; the prices here are fictional.
