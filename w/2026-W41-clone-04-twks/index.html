swiss design studio

Independent design studio based in Lausanne since 2012. We work with local and international brands and institutions on their identity, digital presence and campaigns.

What we do

When every brand follows the same codes, they all end up looking alike. The ones that last are built on what is true about them, not on what is expected. We work with clients who want that. Together we build brands people recognise and keep. Brands you remember, not only see.
