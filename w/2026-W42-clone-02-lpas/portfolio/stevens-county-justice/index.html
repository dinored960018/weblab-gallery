Stevens County Justice Center

Stevens County Justice Center combines four courtrooms, the clerk and the prosecutor in one building.

Public, staff and in-custody routes never cross. Courtrooms have daylight from clerestory windows.

The public lobby faces the main street with a covered entry.

Client: Stevens County · Area: 74,000 sf · Courtrooms: 4
