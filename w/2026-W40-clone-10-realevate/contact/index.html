Terms of Use for the Halvoria Website

These terms apply to the website of Halvoria (“Halvoria” or “the agency”). Using the website means accepting these terms. If you do not accept them, please stop using the website.

1. Definitions

a. “User” – any person or company using the website.

b. “Properties” – real estate shown on the website and the information about it.

c. “Services” – information, referral, and contact services offered through the website.

2. The Service

a. The website shows general information about property for sale in Portugal, Croatia, and Albania, including photos, descriptions, technical data, and prices.

b. Halvoria markets projects for developers and advises buyers. It works with a small number of local developers and presents their projects here and in other channels.

c. Halvoria can arrange viewings, explain projects, and accompany buyers during the purchase.

d. Any purchase is a direct agreement between the buyer and the seller. Halvoria is not a party to it.

e. A listing on the website is not a binding offer.

f. Travel, accommodation, and insurance costs for viewings are paid by the buyer unless agreed otherwise in writing.

3. Investment Risk

a. Buying property abroad carries economic, planning, legal, and tax risk.

b. Information on the website comes from third parties and may be incomplete or out of date.

c. The website is not legal, tax, or investment advice. Consult your own advisors before any decision.

4. Liability

a. Halvoria is not responsible for the accuracy of third-party information or for the conduct of developers.

b. Prices and availability can change without notice.

5. Fees

a. Halvoria is paid by developers. Buyers do not pay Halvoria a commission.

6. Privacy

a. Details sent through the contact form are used only to answer the request and to send occasional updates, if you agree to them.

b. You can ask for your details to be deleted at any time by email.
