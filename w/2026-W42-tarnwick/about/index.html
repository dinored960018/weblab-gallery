About this site

A demonstration site. The canal, the locks and the keepers are invented.

Notice

  1. Tarnwick, the Tarnwick Canal, its locks and its keepers are invented for a design exercise.
  2. Times, tolls and closures are fixed for Thursday 15 October 2026, 09:00 BST.
  3. Nothing is booked, paid or sent. Inputs stay in the address bar.
  4. Do not use these figures on a real canal.

Figures

Chamber sizes
In the range of English narrow canals: 21 to 22 m by 2.1 to 2.2 m.
Widest boat
Chamber width less 0.07 m.
Deepest draught
Depth over the cill less 0.10 m.
Time per lock
Fill time (empty time is 1 min less) plus 5 min for the gates.
Time between locks
Pound length at 40 m a minute.
Boats ahead
18 min each: one turn at the slowest lock.
Water per fill
Chamber length × width × fall, rounded to the cubic metre.
Drawings
Section and profile are drawn from the figures on each page; heights are exaggerated about twice.
