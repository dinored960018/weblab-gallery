Privacy Policy

Updated 01 · SEP · 26

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and message when you write to us through a form on this site.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, kept in aggregate.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and to plan a project with you.
  • To improve the site. We do not sell or rent personal data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for two years, or for the length of a project plus seven years for accounting records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time by writing to privacy@parlorofplum.com.

Cookies

This site stores a single session flag in your browser so the opening screen plays only once per visit. It is removed when you close the tab.
