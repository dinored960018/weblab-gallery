Midnight Garden

Parlor of Plum in Montecito: Midnight Garden

Dark rooms that glow

A garden house for night owls. The clients wanted rooms that feel best after sunset.

We painted the walls in inky green and aubergine, then added low lamps, leather chairs and a round table built for four. Art hangs lower than usual so it meets you at the table. Daylight still gets in; it just has to ask.
