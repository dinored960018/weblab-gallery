The Bloom

A Chair for the Road: The Wren Lounge, Travel Edition

Our lounge chair, packed for a trip

The Wren Lounge started as a chair for one client. It now travels in a limited run covered in a hand-printed map fabric made with a small mill in Portugal.

Each chair ships with a leather luggage tag stitched to the back and a numbered certificate.

Notes

More on the project

The frame is solid walnut, the seat is hand-tied, and the fabric is heavy enough for a house with children and cats.

Twenty-five chairs, made to order in Los Angeles.
