What to Pack for a Hut Walk

(Details)

Introduction

A hut walk means no tent and no stove, but the pack still needs care. Huts supply beds and meals; you carry clothes, water, and a few small items.

(Highlights)

Things to Try

  • Boots You Know
  • Bring boots that are already worn in. New boots on a three-day walk mean blisters by day two.
  • A Sleeping Liner
  • Most huts need a light sheet liner. It weighs less than 200 grams.
  • Layers, Not Bulk
  • One warm fleece and one shell jacket cover most alpine weather in summer.
  • Cash for the Hut
  • Many mountain huts take cash only for drinks and snacks.
(Perspective)

The Varnelo View

We send a pack list two weeks before each walk. Guides check packs on the first morning and swap anything too heavy.

A pack under eight kilos makes steep sections easier for everyone in the group.

(Final Thought)

Final Thought

Pack less than you think. The hut has the rest.
