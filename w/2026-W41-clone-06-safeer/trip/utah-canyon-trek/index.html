Utah Canyon Trek

(Details)

Trip Overview

Utah Canyon Trek visits three national parks in five days. Canyon rims, slot canyons, and arches, with short daily hikes and two nights of camping under the stars.

(Schedule)

Trip Schedule

Duration:
5 Days / 4 Nights
Trip Type:
Canyon Hiking
Group Size:
Max 10 People
Price:
$1,350/per person
Meeting Point:
Las Vegas Airport
Destination:
Utah, USA
(Includes)

Included in This Package

  • Van transport from Las Vegas
  • Two lodge and two camp nights
  • All meals on camp days
  • Park passes and guide
(Experience)

What You’ll Experience

  • Walk the rim at Island in the Sky
  • Enter a slot canyon with a guide
  • Hike to a sandstone arch
  • Camp night with a star talk
(Itinerary)

Canyon Days

  • Day 1Drive to Zion and evening walk
  • Day 2Slot canyon with a guide
  • Day 3Canyon rim and camp night
  • Day 4Arches and sunset viewpoint
(Additional Info)

Things to Keep in Mind

Summer days can pass 38 degrees. Hikes start early and finish before noon. Bring a sun hat, three litres of water, and shoes with good grip.
