Madeira Levada Walk

(Details)

Trip Overview

Madeira Levada Walk follows the old water channels of the island over four days. Laurel forest, sea cliffs, and mountain passes, with one hotel in Funchal for the whole stay.

(Schedule)

Trip Schedule

Duration:
4 Days / 3 Nights
Trip Type:
Trail Walking
Group Size:
Max 12 People
Price:
$890/per person
Meeting Point:
Funchal Airport
Destination:
Madeira, Portugal
(Includes)

Included in This Package

  • Airport transfers
  • Hotel in Funchal
  • Daily breakfast and packed lunch
  • Walking guide and transport
(Experience)

What You’ll Experience

  • Walk the Caldeirao Verde levada
  • Cross the Sao Lourenco peninsula
  • Hike between the two highest peaks
  • Dinner in the old town of Funchal
(Itinerary)

Trail Days

  • Day 1Arrival and old town walk
  • Day 2Laurel forest levada
  • Day 3Sao Lourenco coast trail
  • Day 4Pico do Arieiro sunrise walk
(Additional Info)

Things to Keep in Mind

Some levada paths are narrow with drops on one side. A head torch is needed for the tunnels. Bring walking poles and a light rain jacket.
