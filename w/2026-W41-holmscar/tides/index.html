Reading the heights

Chart datum is about the level of the lowest tide. Depths on the chart are below it; heights of tide are above it.

depth now = charted depth + height of tide

On the sill: water over the sill = height of tide − 1.9 m. The board at the sill reads this figure directly.

This week

Neap tides on Sun 4 October. Ranges grow each day to spring tides on Mon 12 October, a day after the new moon. Spring low waters fall below 1 m; the sill dries for about five hours around each one.

Weather

Strong south-westerly winds and low pressure can raise the level by 0.2 to 0.3 m. High pressure lowers it.
