Berth fees, 2026–27

Two seasons. Winter: 1 October to 31 March. Summer: 1 April to 30 September. Charged by the night the boat is in the harbour.

Practice site. Fees are made up.

How the price is worked out

  • Length overall, rounded up to the next 0.5 m. Minimum 8 m.
  • Multihulls ×1.5 on pontoon, marina and wall berths.
  • Every 7th night free. Shore power is charged every night.
  • A night across 31 March or 30 September is charged at the rate of that night.
  • Shore power £4.50 a night where fitted.

Example

A 9.6 m yacht, 3 nights on the visitor pontoon from Mon 5 October.

10.0 m × £2.40 × 3 = £72.00
