Harbour office

Harbour Office, Fish Quay
Holmscar

Counter open 08:00–17:00, Monday to Saturday. Berth fees, door codes, keys and lost property.

Emergency at sea: Ch 16 or 999, ask for the Coastguard.

From abroad

Fly flag Q from the 12-mile limit until you are cleared.

Report your arrival to Border Force before you land. The harbour office can explain the form but cannot clear you.

Signal flags you may see here

  • AI have a diver down; keep well clear at slow speed.
  • BI am taking in, discharging or carrying dangerous goods.
  • GI require a pilot.
  • HI have a pilot on board.
  • OMan overboard.
  • PIn harbour: all persons should report on board as the vessel is about to proceed to sea.
  • QMy vessel is healthy and I request free pratique.
