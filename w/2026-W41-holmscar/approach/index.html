Approach and lights

Enter on the leading line, 312°, from the fairway buoy. The channel is dredged to 2.0 m and open at all states of tide.

Pilotage

  1. From the westPass south of Holm Scar by at least 0.3 nautical miles. Rill Sands dry 2 m and extend 0.6 nautical miles from the shore.
  2. Fairway buoyCall Holmscar Harbour on Ch 12. Turn onto 312° with the leading lights in line.
  3. ChannelBuoys No. 3 to 6. Dredged 2.0 m. Keep to starboard; fishing boats leave at all hours.
  4. Breakwater headsEntrance 60 m wide. Strong cross-set on the ebb, up to 1.5 knots east to west.

Dangers

  • Holm Scar: rock awash at low water springs, 0.2 nautical miles around the light.
  • Sunken mooring block off the slipway, 0.4 m at datum. Yellow buoy. Notice 16/2026.
  • Dredger in the channel, Mon 12 to Fri 23 October. Notice 22/2026.
  • Swell breaks on the bar off Rill Sands in south-westerly gales.
