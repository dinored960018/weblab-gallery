The sums

  • m³ = thickness × width × length × boards, in metres, on the sawn size
  • board feet = m³ × 423.78
  • weight = m³ × density (air-dried stock about 6 % heavier)
  • planing = linear metres × £0.85 up to 200 mm wide, £1.40 over; minimum £20
