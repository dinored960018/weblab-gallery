The lightest timber in the yard: a 4.8 m cladding board weighs under 3 kg.

Use it for

  • Cladding and shingles
  • Sauna benches
  • Anything that has to be light

Avoid

  • Floors and worktops — a fingernail dents it
  • Plain steel fixings — they stain and corrode
