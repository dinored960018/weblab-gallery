The Final Grill

Crowned at full time

FoodPush2026

The Final Grill runs a street food contest that tours stadium car parks. For its final, held in a football ground after the season ended, it needed a campaign that could fill the stands with people who had never heard of the contest, in three weeks.

We treated the burger like a trophy. Key visuals show it under floodlights, lifted on a podium, carried through an empty tunnel. The same frames ran on posters, screens and ticketing, with one line of copy.

Every burger was shot for real, on a single grill, then placed into stadium scenes built from generated plates and lit to match the floodlights of the real ground. Four scenes, one week, and a grill that stayed lit through every take. The fries got their own podium shot.

The final sold out nine days before the gates opened. The trophy frame became the contest’s poster for the next tour.

Building new brands

We give a new company a brand it can explain in one sentence.

Every new company picks a name and a logo early. Most skip the part where they decide what they stand for, and end up with a brand that says little.

Our Building method starts earlier, with the gap between what the company is on day one and what it plans to become.

Building covers

Every company starts from a different place, so the program is set by what the brand needs.

  • → Audience and positionIf the brand walked into a room, who would it talk to first? That answer filters every later choice.
  • → Offer and nameWhat you do that others do not, and a name that holds up on a shelf and in a sales call.
  • → Visual identityA system, not only a logo. Type, colour, motion and images that work wherever the brand turns up.
  • → Tools for your teamTemplates, generators and short rules, so your team can use the brand from the first week without us.

What Building leaves you

A brand your team can describe in one line, use without friction, and grow for years without starting over.

Reworking existing brands

We close the gap between what your brand was and what the company is now.

Brands rarely fail at once. They drift: the company grows, the market moves, and the brand stays where it was left.

Our Reworking method keeps what people already recognise, fixes what no longer fits, and moves the brand to where the company stands today.

Reworking covers

Every rework starts from a different place, so the program is set by what the brand needs.

  • → Audit and diagnosisWho the brand is today and who it has to be. The result is a decision on what stays and what goes, not a report.
  • → Position and messageHow the brand describes itself: offer, tone and key lines. Words your team can repeat in a meeting.
  • → Visual system updateCurrent without losing recognition. Type, colour, motion and images updated for every place the brand appears.
  • → Rollout toolsA rework only works when it replaces the old brand everywhere. Templates, guides and generators so it sticks.

What Reworking leaves you

A brand that looks like the company you are now. Familiar to people who know you, clear to people who do not yet.

Pushing established brands

We help a known brand perform on the dates that count.

A strong brand is not the same as a brand in motion. Launches, campaigns and anniversaries ask it to move fast without losing what it stands for.

Our Pushing method turns an identity into a campaign: one clear idea, made at the speed the date demands.

Pushing covers

Every campaign has its own goal and deadline, so the program is set by the date.

  • → Idea and directionOne idea that can carry the whole campaign, rooted in what the brand already is.
  • → Key visuals and assetsLead images and the system around them, ready for every size and channel.
  • → Motion and image productionFilm, stills and generated backgrounds made by a small crew at the pace of the calendar.
  • → Rollout for channelsCut-downs, formats and handover files, so every channel launches on the same day.

What Pushing leaves you

A campaign that lands on time and looks like your brand, not like the season’s trend.
