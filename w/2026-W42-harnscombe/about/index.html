About this site

A demonstration site. The centre, its towers and its people are invented.

Notice

  1. Harnscombe, its tower, the 24 nearby towers and every name on this site are invented. Any match with a real place is chance.
  2. Bell weights, keys, steps and practice nights are made-up values in the range of rural towers in south-west England.
  3. Nothing on this site is sent anywhere. Bookings and course places are kept in this browser tab only and vanish when it closes.

Figures

Rows
Generated from place notation by the usual rule: bells in the named places stay, every other pair swaps.
Bell weights
Each bell in a ring is scaled from the tenor by its pitch: weight × (frequency ratio)^−1.6. A rough figure, not a founder’s record.
Estimator
Weight = k × diameter³, with k = 0.00047, 0.00056 or 0.00066 kg/cm³ for light, medium and heavy profiles. Nominal pitch ≈ 75 850 ÷ diameter Hz, adjusted by profile.
Duration
Total = changes × seconds per change. Gap between blows = change time ÷ bells, or 2 × change time ÷ (2 × bells + 1) with the open handstroke lead.
Units
1 cwt = 112 lb = 4 qr; 1 qr = 28 lb; 1 lb = 0.4536 kg.
