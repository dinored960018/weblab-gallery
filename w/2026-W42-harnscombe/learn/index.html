Questions

How fit do I need to be?

Ringing is a matter of timing, not strength. You need to climb 72 steps and stand for an hour and a half with short breaks.

What does the fee cover?

£48 covers six evenings, a tutor on every rope and a printed handbook. The simulator hour on Tuesdays is free while you are on the course.

What if I miss a week?

Come to the Tuesday simulator hour that week; the tutor will cover the missed stroke work before the next Thursday.

I am left-handed.

It makes no difference. Both hands hold the sally and the tail in the same way.

What comes after the course?

Wednesday tower practice. Most learners ring in rounds for a service within three months and plain hunt within six.
