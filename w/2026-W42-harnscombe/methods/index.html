Reading it

  • x — every pair of bells swaps places.
  • 16 — the bells in 1st and 6th place stay; the pairs between swap.
  • Every bell hunts out to the back and in to the lead. Two blows at each end.
  • The treble plain hunts. At each lead end the bell in 2nds makes second’s place and the others dodge.
  • Each column is one lead. Its last row is the first row of the next.
