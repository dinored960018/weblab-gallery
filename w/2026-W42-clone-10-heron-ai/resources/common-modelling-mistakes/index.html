Common modelling mistakes

The errors that cost the most time are rarely dramatic. They are small and repeated.

Five to look for

Rooms without numbers. Doors hosted to the wrong wall. Duplicate types with one letter changed. Levels copied instead of shared. Sheets with no revision.

Fix the source

Each of these has a cause upstream. Fix the template and the mistake stops appearing in new projects.
