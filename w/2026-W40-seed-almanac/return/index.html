Returning seed

Due by 30 November. A packet that did not grow still comes back — tell us what happened.

  1. 1

    Choose

    Mark the healthiest plants before harvest starts. Never save from the first plant to bolt.

  2. 2

    Ripen

    Leave seed on the plant until pods rattle, heads turn brown or fruit is past eating ripe.

  3. 3

    Clean

    Dry seed: rub heads over a sieve and blow off chaff. Wet seed (tomato, cucumber, squash): scoop, rinse, and for tomato ferment 3 days first.

  4. 4

    Dry

    7–14 days on paper or a plate, 20–25 °C, out of direct sun. Beans snap rather than bend when dry.

  5. 5

    Label

    Variety, year, where grown, number of plants, anything that crossed nearby.

  6. 6

    Return

    Paper envelope to the drop box, or in person on the first Sunday of the month.

Testing

Ten seeds from each lot go on damp paper for 14 days. Lots under 70% germination are composted, not lent. The grower hears the result.
