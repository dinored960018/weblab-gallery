Notes

Lime-green double flowers. Dislikes cold and root disturbance — sow in modules.

Saving seed · Moderate

Insects cross it with other varieties. Grow one variety, isolate, or bag and hand-pollinate.

  1. Grow one variety of this plant, or keep 50 m from others.
  2. Or bag three flowers before they open and hand-pollinate.
  3. Leave the seed to ripen fully, then dry 7–14 days.
