Notes

Early dwarf pea, 45 cm. Late sowings in July reach harvest before frost in most years.

Saving seed · Easy

Self-pollinating annual. Seed ripens the same season. Keep 3 m from other varieties of the same plant.

  1. Mark two or three healthy plants early.
  2. Leave the fruit, pod or head to ripen fully on the plant.
  3. Dry the seed 7–14 days on paper, out of direct sun.
