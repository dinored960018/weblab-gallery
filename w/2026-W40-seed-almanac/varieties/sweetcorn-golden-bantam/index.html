Notes

Yellow sweetcorn, grown in blocks for wind pollination. Needs about 100 plants to keep the variety healthy — return as a shared lot.

Saving seed · Hard

Biennial or wind-pollinated. Seed comes in the second year or needs 20+ plants to stay true.

  1. Grow at least 20 plants of this variety.
  2. Biennials: store or overwinter roots, replant in March.
  3. Tell us before you start — we pool hard lots between members.
