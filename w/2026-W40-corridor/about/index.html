About

Night trains between eight countries. Four routes, eight departures a night, one ticket per journey.

Trains leave in the evening and arrive before 09:00. Cars are cleaned and restocked at the depots in Köln and Wien during the day.

This site

CORRIDOR is fictional, built as a design exercise. Stations are real. The operator, train numbers, times, fares and seat counts are invented.
