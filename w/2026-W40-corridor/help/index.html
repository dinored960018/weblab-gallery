Help

At the first station, 30 minutes before departure. Boarding closes 2 minutes before.

Doors open on request. Tell the attendant when you board which station you leave at, and you will be woken 30 minutes before.

Two cases and one small bag per person. Racks above the corridor in Seat and Couchette; under the lower berth in Sleeper.

In the seat car only, 6 per train. €12 per bicycle, booked with the ticket.

In a Sleeper compartment booked whole. €20 per dog, one dog per compartment.

Under 4 travel free without their own berth. 4–11 pay 50%. From 12, adult fare.

Free changes up to 24 hours before departure. Refund minus €10 up to 3 days before. After that, no refund.

Over 60 minutes: 25% back. Over 120 minutes: 50% back. Claim within 3 months, ticket number required.
