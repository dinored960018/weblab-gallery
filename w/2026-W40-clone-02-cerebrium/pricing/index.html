Pricing FAQ

How do per-second prices compare with hourly cloud instances?

Hourly instances bill for boot time, idle time and the headroom you keep for peaks. Our containers start in 1-3 seconds and stop when traffic stops, so the billable time is close to the time spent on requests.

The price also covers orchestration, networking and the routing layer. For uneven traffic the monthly total is usually lower even when the per-second rate is higher.

Show me a billing example

An A10 endpoint with 1 vCPU and 8 GB memory costs $0.000313 per second. At 40,000 requests a month and 1.5 seconds each, that is 60,000 seconds, or $18.78.

Can I use cloud credits?

Yes, on Enterprise plans we can draw on credits you hold with the major clouds. Ask us for the list of supported programs.

Are there discounts for volume or longer terms?

Yes. Committed monthly spend and 12-month terms both lower the per-second rate. Talk to us with your expected usage.

Can I get guaranteed capacity without reserving nodes?

Enterprise plans can include a capacity floor per region. You still pay per second for what runs; the floor only guarantees availability.
