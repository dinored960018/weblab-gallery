OUR LODGES& CAMPS

Three lodges and three camps on the Lehana River, each with its own character.

Lodges

Selowe River Lodge and Ashby's Lodge offer privacy, space and considered interiors. Every suite faces the Lehana River, where the water draws the widest range of animals and birds on the reserve.

Plunge pools, outdoor showers and long decks put guests close to the bush; game drives stay small, with no more than six guests to a vehicle.

Camps

Kavari Camp, Ebony Camp and Hartley's Camp keep the older safari rhythm: thatch, verandas and a shared table.

Each sits on the river or a seasonal pan, with communal spaces built for long evenings and rooms that open straight onto the bush.

Choosing the right lodge or camp

Lodges have private plunge pools and larger suites. Camps are smaller, older and more communal, with shared verandas and dining.

Kavari Camp and Hartley's Camp have family rooms and a private vehicle for children under twelve.

Between 14 and 20 guests. Ebony Camp takes 14 and can be booked for exclusive use.

Six are open: Selowe River Lodge, Ashby's Lodge, Kavari Camp, Ebony Camp and Hartley's Camp, with Nandi River Lodge opening in 2027.
