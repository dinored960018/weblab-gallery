TERMS &CONDITIONS

Updated 1 March 2026

Bookings

A booking is confirmed when we receive a 30 percent deposit. The balance is due 60 days before arrival.

Cancellation

Cancellations more than 60 days before arrival lose the deposit. Within 60 days the full amount is charged.

Children

Children aged six and over are welcome at all properties. Walks are for guests aged sixteen and over.

Activities

Game drives and walks carry risk. Guests must follow the instructions of their guide at all times.

Rates

Rates are per person per night sharing and include meals, local drinks, two activities a day and the conservation levy.

Liability

We are not liable for loss or damage to personal belongings. Travel insurance is required.
