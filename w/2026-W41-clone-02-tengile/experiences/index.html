EXPERIENCES& ACTIVITIES

Drives, walks, birding, night skies and rest. Planned each day with your guide.

Our guides take you across the oldest paths of this open land, by vehicle and on foot.

Two drives a day are the base of every stay: one at first light, one into the evening. In between there are walks, hides, the pool and long lunches.

Every vehicle seats six. Children under twelve drive in a private vehicle.

Field Notes

Our guides read the bush as it happens. Tracks in the sand, alarm calls in the trees, vultures circling over a kill. Each sign adds to the story around you.

Most guides grew up within sight of the reserve and trained here for two years before taking guests out.

OURACTIVITIES

1

Game Drives

This stretch of the river holds lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo and rhino all year. Selowe guests drive 11 600 hectares; Kavari guests drive 14 900 hectares of open land.

Drives leave before sunrise and in the late afternoon, three to four hours each, with a stop for coffee or a drink.

2

Birding

Riverine forest and open savanna hold over 420 recorded species. Migrants arrive from November to April, including carmine bee-eaters and woodland kingfishers.

Birding drives and hide sessions can be booked with a specialist guide.

3

Bush Walks

On foot, the small things show: dung beetles, termite towers, tracks of the night before. Walks run for two to three hours with an armed guide and a tracker.

Walks are for guests aged sixteen and over.

4

Spa

Treatments in your suite or in the spa pavilion, using oils and clays made by a cooperative in the Mavhu villages.

Book a day ahead through the lodge manager.

5

Night Skies

With no town lights for sixty kilometres, the Milky Way is clear most nights from May to September. Evening drives run on into the dark with a red spotlight.

A star bed on the hide deck can be booked at Kavari Camp.

6

Tennis

Hartley's Camp keeps a grass-and-clay court behind the old stables, with rackets and balls on loan.

Open from 10:00 to 15:00.

Game Drives

  • Open vehicles, six guests
  • Guide and tracker on every drive
  • Two vehicles per sighting
  • Night drives with a red light

Drives follow the light. The morning drive leaves before sunrise, when cats are still moving; the evening drive runs into the dark, when the reserve changes hands to the night animals. Guides plan each route with the tracker team after the morning radio round.
